# Summary (short)

Analog Twin lets you click a site on the Moon or Mars and see the places on Earth with the most similar terrain shape,
rendered under the same sunlight, with the time of year when Earth's light best matches. It also works from Earth to
the Moon and Mars, searches the lunar south pole for base sites from NASA's own maps, draws the Earth to Moon geometry,
and has a small crew of AI agents draft a field campaign from the measured numbers. It uses open NASA, USGS and NOAA
data, and states its limits on screen. One of our checks did not pass: established analog sites do not rank better
than random places by terrain shape alone, and we say so.
